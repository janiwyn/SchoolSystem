## 🖨️ How to Save as PDF / Print

1. Open [http://localhost/SchoolSystem/receipt.php](http://localhost/SchoolSystem/receipt.php) in your web browser.
2. Click the dark **"Print Receipt"** button at the bottom of the page (or press `Ctrl + P`).
3. Set Destination to **"Save as PDF"** in your browser's print dialog to download a PDF copy.
